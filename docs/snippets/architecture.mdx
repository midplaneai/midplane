![An AI agent connects over MCP to Midplane's relay; its queries and results are encrypted end to end, and the relay forwards them without being able to read them. In your network, the Midplane gateway checks, records and runs each statement, writes it to the audit log before it runs, and queries Postgres as its own role. Both of its other connections are outbound: to the relay, which agents' traffic comes in over, and to Midplane Cloud, which gets the query log with values removed and sends signed policies and approvals. Midplane Cloud gets no query results and no credentials.](/images/architecture.png)

On [your computer](/gateway/deploy#your-computer) or at [your own
URL](/gateway/deploy#your-own-url), agents connect to the gateway directly,
with no relay.
