The gateway opens its own connections: to your databases, to Midplane Cloud
and, behind the relay, to the relay. Neither Midplane Cloud nor the relay
connects to the gateway or to your databases.

## The parts

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Gateway" icon="server">
    One process in your network, next to your databases. It holds their
    connection strings, checks every statement, and keeps the audit log.
    Agents talk only to it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Databases" icon="database">
    Each has an id, such as `shop`, and its own policy: which tables agents may
    use, and what comes back masked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Agents" icon="robot">
    MCP clients such as Claude Code or claude.ai. Each signs in through the
    gateway, and you choose which databases it may use.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Relay" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Midplane's, in its region. It gives a gateway in a private network a
    public HTTPS URL, its Midplane URL, and passes agents' encrypted traffic
    to it without reading it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Midplane Cloud" icon="cloud">
    Where you write the policy, approve held writes and read the Query log. It
    signs every policy it sends, and gets no rows and no connection strings.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Project" icon="folder">
    In Midplane Cloud: databases, their policies, their gateways and the agents
    allowed in. Published policies reach every gateway in seconds.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## How agents reach the gateway

| Where the gateway runs | Agents reach it at | Which agents | What you set up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Your computer, this computer only](/gateway/deploy#your-computer) | The `http://gw-….localhost` URL setup prints, with its port | Those on the same computer | Node |
| [A server, a platform or your computer, behind the relay](/gateway/deploy#docker) | Its Midplane URL, such as `https://gw-7hq2m4xd.eu.midplane.app/mcp` | Any, claude.ai and ChatGPT included | Nothing inbound: the gateway connects out on port 443 |
| [A server at your own URL](/gateway/deploy#your-own-url) | Your URL, such as `https://midplane.example.com/mcp` | Any that can reach the URL | A DNS record, and ports 80 and 443 open or a certificate of your own |

Hosted agents, such as claude.ai and ChatGPT, run on their vendor's servers,
so they need a public HTTPS URL. Behind the relay the gateway has one, even in
a private network with nothing open.

### Through the relay

<Steps>
  <Step title="The gateway connects out">
    It keeps 4 connections open to the relay of its region, such as
    `relay.eu.midplane.app`, and authenticates them with the token it uses
    with Midplane Cloud.
  </Step>
  <Step title="An agent connects to the Midplane URL">
    The URL's name, such as `gw-7hq2m4xd.eu.midplane.app`, points at the
    relay. Each gateway gets its own when it joins a project.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The relay passes the bytes on">
    It reads only the name the agent asks for in its TLS handshake, then
    passes the still-encrypted bytes over one of that gateway's connections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="TLS ends at the gateway">
    With a certificate the gateway got from Let's Encrypt, for a key that
    never leaves its disk. The agent's statement then takes the path below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The relay can't read a query, a result or a token, but it is in the path:
while it's down, agents can't reach the gateway, and Midplane controls the
DNS of the names it serves. [Security](/security#the-relay) has what the relay
sees, what it can't, and how to watch for a certificate your gateway didn't
get. To keep Midplane out of the path, give the gateway [your own
URL](/gateway/deploy#your-own-url).

## The path of a statement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check">
    The gateway parses the statement and checks it against the database's
    policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record">
    It writes the attempt and the decision to its audit log, before anything
    runs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run">
    An allowed statement runs, with masks applied inside the query. A held
    write waits for a person's approval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What stays and what goes up

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Stays in your network" icon="lock">
    Connection strings, database error messages, the mask salt, and the full
    audit log. Results go only to your agents.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Goes to Midplane Cloud" icon="cloud-arrow-up">
    Table and column names, statements with their values replaced, gateway and
    database status, and writes held for approval, as written. Full statements
    only if you turn them on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Passes through the relay" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    Agents' traffic, still encrypted. The relay sees the gateway's name, the
    addresses, and when and how many bytes cross, never what they say.
  </Card>
</Columns>
