[Midplane](/) is a gateway between AI agents and your Postgres databases.
ChatGPT queries your data through it, never holds a connection string, and
every statement it sends is checked against your policy before it runs.

ChatGPT connects from OpenAI's servers, so it reaches a gateway only at a
public HTTPS URL. A gateway [on a server with Docker](/gateway/deploy#docker)
or [on a platform](/gateway/deploy#on-a-platform) has one: its Midplane URL,
through Midplane's relay, with no tunnel, DNS record or open port. A gateway
on [your computer](/gateway/deploy#your-computer) has none, as it answers
only on that computer; to use a tunnel or a URL of your own instead, see
[hosted agents](/gateway/hosted-agents).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the gateway's URL">
    On the project's **Agents** page, under **Connect an agent**, pick
    **ChatGPT** and copy the URL, such as
    `https://gw-7hq2m4xd.eu.midplane.app/mcp`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom MCP server">
    In ChatGPT on the web, open **Plugins**, select **+**, then **Add custom
    MCP server**. Name it `Midplane`, paste the URL as the **Server URL**
    under **Connection**, and choose **OAuth** for authentication. Read the
    risk warning, select **I understand and want to continue**, then
    **Create as a plugin**.

    Your workspace's settings may not allow custom MCP servers: ask its
    admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and choose what it may reach">
    Install the plugin. ChatGPT opens Midplane: sign in, set each database to
    **None**, **Read** or **Read + write**, and **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    In a new chat, type `@`, pick `Midplane`, and ask which tables it can
    see. Its statement shows up in the project's **Query log**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You're set: ChatGPT can answer questions from your data, and only what your
  policy allows runs. Masked columns stay masked, writes that need approval
  wait for a person, and every statement is in the audit log. Results go from
  your gateway to OpenAI, never through Midplane Cloud, and the relay can't
  read them ([what the relay sees](/security#the-relay)).
</Check>

## Permissions

ChatGPT runs `query`, `list_tables`, `describe_table` and `check_approval`
without asking: the gateway marks them read-only. It asks you to confirm each
`write` call, and can remember your answer for the rest of the chat; an
agent you gave **Read** on every database has no `write` tool at all.
Midplane's policy decides on top: a write it holds still waits for a project
manager ([what clients ask you to
confirm](/agents/tools#what-clients-ask-you-to-confirm)).

To cut it off, **Revoke** it under **Connected agents** on the **Agents** page.
It has to sign in again within 15 minutes.

## If it doesn't work

- **ChatGPT can't connect to the URL**: check that the setup step says
  **Relay · connected**, or the **Gateways** page **Relay: connected**, or
  see [cannot reach the relay](/troubleshooting#cannot-reach-the-relay).
- **The consent page says it wants access, with no databases**: you aren't a
  member of the gateway's project, or the URL isn't registered. Ask an owner
  or admin of its organization to invite you.
- **Through OpenAI's Secure MCP Tunnel** instead of the relay, signing in
  isn't verified yet: see [hosted agents](/gateway/hosted-agents#chatgpt-and-codex-openai-secure-mcp-tunnel).

*Tested by* `apps/gateway/test/setup-relay.test.ts` (a gateway behind a
stand-in relay) and `apps/gateway/test/auth.test.ts` for the gateway's side;
the client's steps by hand, through the relay.
