[Midplane](/) is a gateway between AI agents and your Postgres databases.
Claude queries your data through it, never holds a connection string, and
every statement it sends is checked against your policy before it runs.

You need a running gateway ([get started](/get-started)). claude.ai runs on
Anthropic's servers, so it reaches the gateway only at a public HTTPS URL.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Give the gateway a public URL">
    Use Anthropic's MCP tunnel (in research preview; for claude.ai it needs
    the Enterprise plan) or your own ingress, such as a load balancer
    allowlisted to Anthropic's IP ranges. [Hosted
    agents](/gateway/hosted-agents) sets up either.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the URL">
    Add it to the gateway's `public_urls`. If the gateway enrolled before you
    added it, press **Register** on the project's **Gateways** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the connector">
    In claude.ai, add a connector with the gateway's public URL followed by
    `/mcp`, such as `https://midplane.<tunnel domain>/mcp`. The **Agents**
    page shows it under **Connect an agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and choose what it may reach">
    Set each database to **None**, **Read** or **Read + write**, and **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    Ask Claude which tables it can see. Its statement shows up in the
    project's **Query log**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You're set: Claude can answer questions from your data, and only what your
  policy allows runs. Masked columns stay masked, writes that need approval
  wait for a person, and every statement is in the audit log. Results go from
  your gateway to Anthropic, never through Midplane Cloud.
</Check>

To cut it off, **Revoke** it under **Connected agents** on the **Agents** page.
It has to sign in again within 15 minutes.

## If it doesn't work

- **The consent page says it wants access, with no databases**: you aren't a
  member of the gateway's project, or the URL isn't registered. Ask an owner
  of the organization.
- **The Gateways page shows the URL as not registered**: see
  [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#not-registered).

*Tested by* `apps/gateway/test/urls.e2e.test.ts` (several URLs, and a tunnel
that rewrites Host) and `apps/gateway/test/auth.test.ts` for the gateway's
side; the client's steps by hand.
