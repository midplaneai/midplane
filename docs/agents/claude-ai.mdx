[Midplane](/) is a gateway between AI agents and your Postgres databases.
Claude queries your data through it, never holds a connection string, and
every statement it sends is checked against your policy before it runs.

claude.ai connects from Anthropic's servers, so it reaches a gateway only at
a public HTTPS URL. A gateway [on a server with Docker](/gateway/deploy#docker)
or [on a platform](/gateway/deploy#on-a-platform) has one: its Midplane URL,
through Midplane's relay, with no tunnel, DNS record or open port. A gateway
on [this machine](/gateway/deploy#this-machine) has none; to use a tunnel or
a URL of your own instead, see [hosted agents](/gateway/hosted-agents).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the gateway's URL">
    On the project's **Agents** page, under **Connect an agent**, pick
    **claude.ai** and copy the URL, such as
    `https://gw-7hq2m4xd.eu.midplane.app/mcp`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a custom connector">
    In claude.ai, go to **Customize > Connectors**, click **+ Add**, then
    **Add custom connector**. Name it `Midplane`, paste the URL and click
    **Continue**. Keep the sign-in settings it detects, and click **Add**.

    On a Team or Enterprise plan, an owner adds it for the organization, in
    **Organization settings > Connectors** (**Add**, then **Custom**, then
    **Web**). Each member then finds it under **Customize > Connectors** and
    clicks **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and choose what it may reach">
    claude.ai opens Midplane. Sign in, set each database to **None**, **Read**
    or **Read + write**, and **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try it">
    In a chat, check that `Midplane` is on under **+ > Connectors**, and ask
    Claude which tables it can see. Its statement shows up in the project's
    **Query log**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
  You're set: Claude can answer questions from your data, and only what your
  policy allows runs. Masked columns stay masked, writes that need approval
  wait for a person, and every statement is in the audit log. Results go from
  your gateway to Anthropic, never through Midplane Cloud, and the relay
  can't read them ([what the relay sees](/security#the-relay)).
</Check>

## Permissions

In the connector's settings, claude.ai groups the gateway's tools: `query`,
`list_tables`, `describe_table` and `check_approval` are read-only, and
`write` is the one write/delete tool. Set the read-only group to **Always
allow** so reads run without a prompt, and keep `write` at **Needs
approval**, or **Blocked** for an agent that should only read: reads keep
working either way. An agent you gave **Read** on every database has no
write/delete tool to configure. Midplane's policy decides on top: a write it
holds still waits for a project manager ([what clients ask you to
confirm](/agents/tools#what-clients-ask-you-to-confirm)).

To cut it off, **Revoke** it under **Connected agents** on the **Agents** page.
It has to sign in again within 15 minutes.

## If it doesn't work

- **claude.ai can't connect to the URL**: check that the gateway's setup step
  or **Gateways** page says **Relay · connected**, or see [cannot reach the
  relay](/troubleshooting#cannot-reach-the-relay).
- **The consent page says it wants access, with no databases**: you aren't a
  member of the gateway's project, or the URL isn't registered. Ask an owner
  of the organization.
- **The Gateways page shows a URL of your own as not registered**: see
  [troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#not-registered).
- **claude.ai won't add another connector**: the Free plan allows one custom
  connector.
