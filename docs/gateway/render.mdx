The gateway runs as a Render background worker with a 1 GB disk, behind
Midplane's relay: agents reach it at a Midplane URL, and the worker has no
port or domain, so nothing connects in. It suits a database Render can
reach over the internet. Background workers need a paid instance type; the
Blueprint picks Starter, in Frankfurt.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an enrollment token">
    In your project, open step 1 of the setup (or **Gateways**, then **Set up
    a gateway**), choose **A server (Docker)**, keep **Midplane URL**, and
    under **Runs on** choose **Render**. Click **Create enrollment token**,
    above the tabs, and keep the page open.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy the Blueprint">
    Click **Deploy to Render**, name the Blueprint, and apply it. Render makes
    the worker and its disk. Its first start says `no config` and stops: it
    has no env file yet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the env file">
    In the worker's **Environment**, choose **Add from .env**, paste the file
    the page shows with each database's connection string after its `=`
    ([prepare your database](/prepare-database)), and save, choosing to
    deploy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it on the page">
    The gateway sets itself up from the environment, joins your project,
    and gets its certificate through the relay. The page then shows its URL
    for agents. The worker's **Logs** show what it says.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run it

- **A connection string changes.** Change it in **Environment** and deploy.
- **Upgrade.** In the worker's **Settings**, change the image's tag to the
  new version, and deploy ([upgrading](/gateway/upgrading#render)). The
  disk, and with it the gateway, stays.
- **Remove it.** Revoke the gateway on your project's **Gateways** page,
  then delete the worker, which deletes its disk with the audit log.

The Blueprint is
[`deploy/render/render.yaml`](https://github.com/midplaneai/midplane/blob/main/deploy/render/render.yaml).

*Tested by* `apps/gateway/test/deploy-templates.test.ts` (one worker, the
version's image, the gateway's folder on its disk, no variable in the
Blueprint).
